Movement changes more than your body. When it becomes a consistent practice, not a task to get through, it changes how you think, how you rest, how you show up. That is what Nura builds toward. Not a program. A practice.

Common questions.

It starts with an application — about five minutes, reviewed personally by Nura. If there’s a fit, she’ll reach out to schedule a call. From there: a thorough intake covering your movement history, lifestyle, and goals. Then your personalized plan, built from the ground up. Ongoing coaching, accountability, and adjustments as you grow — no 12-week cutoff.
Women who want to build strength, improve their health, and create sustainable habits that fit their lives. Primarily women between 30–55 who are balancing careers, families, or demanding lifestyles and want coaching that prioritizes long-term health over quick fixes.
In-person coaching is in Los Angeles. Location details are shared after the intake process. Online coaching is opening soon — join the waitlist to be notified first.
Most trainers begin with programming. NuraMoves begins with understanding. Every recommendation has intention. Every program is built around the individual. The goal isn’t performance improvement alone — it’s a healthier relationship with movement that supports lifelong wellness.
That’s exactly where this starts. The Foundation Method begins with understanding where you are — not where you think you should be. No assumed baseline, no judgment. We build from there.
