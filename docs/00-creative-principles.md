# NuraMoves — Creative Principles
*Synthesized from all five planning documents.*
*Read this before every design, copy, or development decision.*
*Every choice must trace back to one of these principles.*

---

## The Brand in One Line

**Wellness Leads. Beauty Follows.**

This is the north star. Every page, every section, every sentence either supports this belief or it doesn't belong.

---

## The Thread

**Intentional movement** — moving with purpose rather than obligation — runs through every page. It is not about doing more. It is about doing what matters most for the individual. This idea is never stated all at once. It is revealed gradually as the visitor moves through the site.

---

## What This Must Feel Like

A boutique wellness studio. An editorial magazine. A luxury hotel. A thoughtfully designed home.

Not a gym. Not a fitness app. Not a template.

If a section could appear on any fitness website — rebuild it.

---

## The Five Principles

### 1. Photography leads. Always.
Every image has a purpose. Large, editorial, real — coaching moments, movement, details, hands, eye contact, natural light. The interface exists to support photography, never to compete with it. When there is no photography, typography fills that role. There is no third option.

### 2. Whitespace is not empty. It is content.
Breathing room creates pace, emphasis, and emotional weight. Crowding a section is a sign that the content hasn't been edited enough. When in doubt, remove something.

### 3. Typography creates emotion through scale — not decoration.
Two typefaces only. One serif for headlines and editorial moments. One sans-serif for body, UI, and captions. The contrast between an oversized headline and a small caption IS the design. No additional fonts. No decorative type treatments.

### 4. No two sections share the same composition.
Alternate between: full-bleed photography, oversized type, two-column storytelling, pull quotes, quiet text moments, minimal CTAs. The rhythm of the page should feel like a well-edited magazine — not a landing page template.

### 5. Every word earns its place.
Short. Direct. Confident. Intentional. The site reveals ideas rather than explaining everything. If a sentence can be removed without losing meaning, remove it. The visitor should feel like they are discovering the brand — not being sold to.

## Color

- **Burgundy / Deep Maroon** — primary accent, emotional signature
- **Muted Navy** — secondary, used sparingly for trust and professionalism
- **Warm Off-White** — primary background
- **Charcoal** — primary text
- **Warm soft gray** — secondary text only (not cool, not neutral)

Avoid: earthy greens, beige wellness palettes, bright fitness colors, large areas of saturated color.

---

## Motion

Almost invisible. Slow editorial reveals. Sections breathe as they enter. Nothing calls attention to itself. The motion should feel like the page is exhaling — not performing. No bouncing, spinning, or attention-seeking transitions.

---

## What We Never Build

Cards · Icon grids · Feature boxes · Repeating three-column layouts · Unnecessary borders · Decorative elements · Staged gym photography · Generic stock imagery · Sections that explain what the photography already shows

---

## What We Never Write

shred · crush · transform your body · before/after · results in X weeks · high-achieving women (overused) · burn · tone · blast · no excuses · grind · hustle · bikini body

---

## The One Test

Before any section ships:

> Does this feel like a boutique wellness studio — or a fitness app?

If the answer is fitness app: rebuild it.
