# NuraMoves — Coaching Methodology

---

## Naming — Final Definitions

**The Brand Nu Method**
The coaching *philosophy* behind NuraMoves.
It explains *why* Nura coaches the way she does and the beliefs that shape every coaching experience.
This is the worldview.

**The Foundation Method**
The coaching *methodology*.
It explains *how* new clients begin working with Nura.
It is the practical implementation of The Brand Nu Method.
The Foundation Method page should focus on the coaching process and client experience — not repeat the philosophy.

One is the conviction. The other is the curriculum.

---

## The Brand Nu Method — The Philosophy

Built on the belief that intentional movement changes more than the body.

Not a program to white-knuckle through. A framework that fits how a person actually lives — and builds momentum that compounds.

Rooted in:
- Moving with purpose rather than obligation
- Building wellness before chasing aesthetics
- Treating each client as an individual, not a program

---

## The Foundation Method — The Methodology

The process every new NuraMoves client moves through.

The Foundation Method page exists to answer: *What actually happens when you start working with Nura?*

### Phase 1 — Understand
Learn the client's movement history, lifestyle, goals, and what has gotten in the way before. No assumptions. No generic starting points.

### Phase 2 — Build Foundation
Movement quality first. Confidence second. Load and intensity third. No unnecessary soreness. No grinding through pain.

### Phase 3 — Layer Structure
Accountability, check-ins, and adjustments as life shifts. Structure that adapts rather than rigidly demands.

### Phase 4 — Sustain
No 12-week cutoff. The work evolves as the client does. The goal is a practice that keeps giving back.
