# NuraMoves — Creative Direction
*This is not a design system. It is the art direction.*
*Mood, atmosphere, and feeling — before any design decision is made.*

---

## Feeling

The website should feel like:
- A boutique wellness studio
- An editorial magazine
- A luxury hotel
- A thoughtfully designed home

It should NOT feel like:
- A gym
- A fitness app
- A medical clinic

---

## Materials

If NuraMoves were physical, it would be made of:

**Yes:**
- Linen
- Matte paper
- Oak
- Brushed metal
- Natural light

**No:**
- Chrome
- Neon
- Rubber gym flooring
- Glossy plastic

---

## Visual Rhythm

The page should move like a well-edited magazine — not a landing page.

Each section has a different composition. The rhythm alternates between:
- Full-bleed photography
- Oversized typography
- Quiet moments
- Small captions
- Pull quotes
- Unexpected whitespace

No two sections should feel the same.

---

## Photography

**Do:**
- Coaching moments
- Movement
- Details
- Hands
- Eye contact
- Natural light
- Candid, not composed

**Don't:**
- Flexing
- Dumbbell smiles
- Stock gym imagery
- Fake high fives
- Staged "before" energy
- Mirror selfies

Photography communicates: confidence, movement, education, connection, intention.

Color photography is primary. Black and white used intentionally and sparingly.

---

## Typography Mood

Not font names. Mood.

Typography should feel quiet, refined, and confident.

Headlines create emotion through scale — not decorative styling.

Large headlines carry feeling. Small captions carry information.

The contrast between sizes IS the design.

---

## Layout Rules

**Never:**
- Cards
- Icon grids
- Feature boxes
- Repeating three-column sections
- Bordered content panels

**Always:**
- Photography drives hierarchy
- Alternating layouts — no two sections the same
- Whitespace is intentional, not empty
- Alignment shifts create movement
- The page should feel composed, not assembled

---

## Motion

Almost invisible.

Slow, editorial reveals. Sections breathe as they enter.

Nothing calls attention to itself. Motion should feel like the page is exhaling — not performing.

No bouncing. No spinning. No attention-seeking transitions.

---

## References

Study these for restraint, spacing, hierarchy, and photography — not for colors or direct style copying.

**Aesop** — restraint, material honesty, copy that trusts the reader
**Apple** — hierarchy through scale, photography as the primary element
**Flamingo Estate** — warmth, editorial photography, unhurried rhythm
**Kinfolk** — whitespace, quiet confidence, the beauty of simplicity
**Cabana Magazine** — varied layouts, photography-led, no two pages the same
**The Gentlewoman** — typographic confidence, editorial intelligence, feminine without softness

---

## One Test

Before any section is built, ask:

> Does this feel like it belongs in a boutique wellness studio or a fitness app?

If the answer is fitness app — redesign it.
