## Content Architecture

### HOME
**Purpose:** Inspire. Make the right person feel seen and want to know more.
**Primary message:** Wellness Leads. Beauty Follows.

Sections:
1. Hero — video, brand line, one CTA
2. Philosophy — The Brand Nu Method, brief
3. Services — three offerings, clean and direct
4. Testimonials — pull quotes, no cards
5. FAQ — process + common questions
6. CTA — minimal, confident

---

### ABOUT
**Purpose:** Build trust. Make Nura feel like a real person, not a brand.
**Primary message:** Coaching Is Personal.

Sections:
1. Hero — who Nura is, one line
2. Story — personal narrative, first person, honest
3. Credentials — education and certification, brief
4. CTA — apply or ask a question

*Remove: Philosophy/Beliefs section (lives on Home), Experience section (redundant with Story)*

---

### COACHING
**Purpose:** Explain what Nura offers. Let the right person self-select.
**Primary message:** Three ways to work together, all built on the same method.

Sections:
1. Hero — framing line
2. 1:1 Coaching — what it is, what's included
3. Small Group Coaching — what it is, what's included
4. Online Coaching — coming soon, waitlist
5. FAQ — service-specific questions
6. CTA

*Remove: Brand Nu Method as a service card, Who This Is For section, Journey Slider, duplicate testimonials*

---

### FOUNDATION METHOD
**Purpose:** Explain how new clients begin. What actually happens.
**Primary message:** Foundation Before Intensity.

Sections:
1. Hero — what the Foundation Method is (one clear sentence)
2. The four phases — Understand → Build → Structure → Sustain
3. What to expect — practical, honest, low-pressure
4. CTA → Apply

*This page is not about philosophy. It is about process and client experience.*

---

### RESULTS
**Purpose:** Remove doubt. Nothing else.
**Primary message:** Real stories from real clients.

Sections:
1. Hero — minimal intro, let the stories speak
2. Full client testimonials — detailed, with context
3. CTA — "These women started exactly where you are."

*Remove: Outcomes grid (the testimonials already show the outcomes)*

---

### APPLY
**Purpose:** Reduce fear. Encourage commitment.
**Primary message:** This is the beginning, not a test.

Sections:
1. Hero — what to expect, low pressure
2. Sidebar — brief expectations (2–3 points max)
3. Form — name, email, goals, history, timeline
4. Submit CTA

---

### CONTACT
**Purpose:** Handle non-coaching inquiries professionally.
**Primary message:** For everything that isn't a coaching application.

Sections:
1. Hero + redirect note (coaching → apply.html)
2. Form — inquiry type, name, email, message
3. Direct links — Instagram, email

## Design System

### Color Palette
| Role | Color |
|---|---|
| Primary accent | Burgundy / Deep Maroon |
| Secondary accent | Muted Navy |
| Background | Warm Off-White |
| Text | Charcoal |
| Supporting | Soft Gray (warm, not cool) |

Avoid: earthy greens, beige wellness palettes, bright fitness colors.

### Typography
Two typefaces only.
- One serif — for headlines, pull quotes, editorial moments
- One sans-serif — for body, UI, captions, labels

Hierarchy comes from scale and spacing, not additional fonts.

### Photography
- Photography leads the design — the interface supports it
- Every image must have a purpose — no decoration
- No generic stock fitness photography
- No staged gym poses
- Communicates: confidence, movement, education, connection, intention
- Color photography is primary
- Black and white used intentionally and sparingly

### Layout Principles
Avoid: repeating card layouts, icon grids, unnecessary borders, repetitive three-column sections.

Prioritize: generous whitespace, varied compositions, alternating layouts, photography-driven sections, editorial pacing.

The website should feel composed, not assembled.

---

## Copy Rules

- Short. Direct. Confident. Intentional.
- Every sentence earns its place.
- Show rather than explain.
- Repeat ideas intentionally — not accidentally.
- The visitor should feel like they are discovering the brand, not being sold to.

**Do not use:** shred, crush, transform your body, before/after, results in X weeks, burn, tone, blast, no excuses, grind, hustle, bikini body, weight loss as a primary goal, "high-achieving women" (overused).
