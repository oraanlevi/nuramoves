The story

I came to movement the way most athletes do — early, naturally, and without thinking much about it. I was a collegiate athlete. My body was something I trained, something I competed with, something I pushed.

After school, life got more complicated. Graduate school. A career. Adult responsibilities stacking on top of each other. And somewhere in the middle of building a life, I stopped moving in the way that had always grounded me.

I didn't just lose my fitness. I lost a version of myself I didn't realize I'd been carrying.

That experience became the foundation for everything NuraMoves is built on. Not because I want to tell that story forever — but because I meet that woman every single week. She's accomplished. She's driven. She's put herself on the back burner for so long that she's not even sure where to start.

I built the Foundation Method because the standard fitness industry approach doesn't work for her. Generic programming. One-size-fits-all intensity. The assumption that if you just "work hard enough" it'll stick. None of that accounts for what her actual life looks like.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Health & Exercise Science from the University of Oklahoma and a Master's degree in Healthcare Administration from UCLA. I'm a certified personal trainer. But the credential that matters most is this: I've worked through what my clients are working through, and I've built something that actually helps.

The goal isn't transformation for its own sake. The goal is feeling like yourself again — and then building from there.

I work with a small number of clients at a time. That's not a limitation — it's the point. This kind of coaching requires presence, attention, and a real relationship. I want to know how your week went, why last Tuesday was hard, what shifted in your body this month. That's how this works.

If you're ready to stop waiting for the right moment and start building something that lasts, I'd love to hear from you.

What I believe

The foundations of the Foundation Method

Movement is medicine.

Not metaphor. Not marketing language. A real, evidence-based understanding that intentional movement changes how your body works, how your mind processes stress, and how you experience your life. When the right kind of movement becomes a consistent practice, everything else gets easier.

Your life is the context, not the obstacle.

Most programs treat your schedule, your stress, and your responsibilities as things to overcome. The Foundation Method treats them as the actual environment we're working within. There's no ideal version of your life that we're waiting on. We build inside the one you have.

Results are felt before they're seen.

Energy. Sleep quality. How you carry yourself walking into a room. These changes come before the mirror does — and they matter more. I want you to feel the difference first. The visible results will follow a practice that's actually working.

Sustainability beats intensity.

The most effective program is one you'll still be doing in six months. That means meeting you at your current capacity, building gradually, and making adjustments as your life shifts. Intensity for its own sake burns people out. Intelligent progression builds something lasting.

Background

Where the work comes from

Before founding NuraMoves, Nura spent years working in healthcare administration — which gave her a rigorous understanding of how bodies actually work, how behavior changes happen (and don't), and why most wellness interventions fail to create lasting impact.

Her athletic background as a collegiate athlete gave her an experiential understanding of training, recovery, and peak performance. Her personal experience navigating seasons of stress and disconnection gave her empathy for exactly the clients she now serves.

The combination is rare: someone who understands movement science, has lived at a high level of athletic performance, knows how healthcare systems think about wellness — and has also been the person who needed to find her way back.

That's the practitioner behind the Foundation Method. And that's why it works.
